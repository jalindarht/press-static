About

A press built around factory deadlines

Shivansh Printers runs out of Koregaon Bhima on the Pune–Ahmadnagar corridor, a short drive from most of the plants we supply. The work is mostly industrial: identification tags, in-out registers, gate passes, production reports, barcode labels and the safety signage a plant audit asks for.

Keeping offset, digital, screen, flexo, punching, binding and board fabrication in the same building is the point. A job does not sit in a queue at someone else’s workshop between two steps, which is what usually turns a two-day order into a two-week one.
